# Hockey audit pre-registration (2026-11-13)

Written before any test below was run. Seen so far: the live model's stored baseline (reproduced), the join rate of the
new odds archive to the game history and its agreement with ESPN closes (corr .998 on 3,903 games), sample payloads of
the two NHL endpoints. No outcome of any test below has been computed.

Lockbox: 2026-27 is not read. Tune and held splits are the game model's own (held 2024-26 is a reused split: a pass
here is scored once more on 2026-27 at season end).

## New data
- SBR: sportsbookreviewsonline.com NHL archive, 2007-08 to 2022-23: opening and closing moneyline, opening and closing
  total with prices. Joined to scripts/nhl_games_hist.json.gz by date (+-1 day) and clubs. Used as the closing consensus
  `c` for seasons 2011-2019 only (ESPN stays the source from 2019-20, as live).
- RR: api-web.nhle.com gamecenter right-rail, every game 2010-11 to 2025-26: referees, head coaches, power plays.
- EDGE: api-web.nhle.com edge/skater-detail, 2021-22 to 2025-26: top skating speed, 20+ mph bursts, top shot speed,
  offensive-zone time share.

## Tests (one Holm family of 6, one-sided, family alpha .05)
Win tests: walk-forward log loss, add-one on the live inputs [elo, xg, b2b, home, rv], paired, clustered by day
(nhl_games.evidence with every game lined, first scored season 2017). Totals tests: walk-forward OLS squared error on
total goals, paired, clustered by day, scored on lined games 2021-26.
Ship rule for A1-B2: tune t >= 1.5 (helps) AND held 2024-26 one-sided p passes Holm.

- A1 `mkt`: the model's market rating (earlier closing moneylines, never the game's own; mhl 80, mcar .2 as stored),
  with SBR closes 2011-19 behind it. Tune 2017-23, held 2024-26.
- A2 `nc`: new coach. A club is flagged when tonight's head coach is not its coach in its first game of the season
  and he has coached it fewer than 20 games this season before tonight. nc = home flag - away flag. Tune 2017-23,
  held 2024-26.
- B1 totals trained on 2016+ (SBR closes feed the market total rating from 2011 and the regression from 2016) against
  the live model (regression on ESPN-lined games 2020+ only). Same inputs [xgT, gT, mtot, gkT, one]. Tune 2021-23,
  held 2024-26.
- B2 `refPP`: referee whistle rate added to the B1 totals design. Per referee before each game: games worked since
  2010-11 (n) and his mean power-play opportunities a game (both clubs) minus the league mean of the same season to
  date (previous season's mean in a season's first 100 games), shrunk by n / (n + 80). refPP = mean over tonight's two
  referees (a referee with no history counts 0). Tune 2021-23, held 2024-26.
- C1 EDGE in the skater projection. Rows: skaters with 400+ minutes in season S (2022-2025) and in S+1. Target:
  next season's single-season rate, the live pWAR weights (data/nhl_war_fit.json: evf, eva, a1, ixg) on S+1 components.
  Base: OLS on the season file's projected HPM after S, age, age squared, D. Candidate: base + four EDGE inputs of
  season S, z-scored within season and F / D: top speed, 20+ mph bursts per 60, top shot speed, offensive-zone share.
  Leave-one-target-season-out (4 folds), squared error weighted by S+1 minutes, paired, clustered by player.
  Pass: t >= 2, better in 3 of 4 folds, Holm. A pass goes on to the pWAR gate and the rv game gate before any ship.
- D1 totals at the open (research; a pass makes NHL totals a paper-pick candidate, nothing more). Model total = B1
  walk-forward total. Bet over when model - opening total >= X, under when <= -X, at SBR's opening price for that
  side, pushes refunded. X from {0.2, 0.3, 0.4, 0.5} = the largest t of return on 2017-2020; verdict on 2021-23 (SBR
  ends Jan 2023) at that X only. Pass: validation return > 0 with t >= 2, n >= 300, Holm.
