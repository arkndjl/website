ESEA League · North America · jersey branding guide

1NT Night, 2026

One jersey, Night, built from the team avatar: a black shirt with the wireframe globe printed across it, the glowing lilac equator as its band, and the orbit logo as the chest mark. Every rule below exists so it reads as 1NT at stream resolution, 720p in a corner of a webcam frame.

01_mark{{ $win | safeHTML }}

01

Mark

Flat colour, one bold silhouette, a sharp swoosh and negative space instead of outlines, then drawn cell by cell on a 64-cell grid, so it reads like the site and holds in one colour; everything in it comes from the original 1NT avatar on FACEIT (shown last), kept as the team's heritage mark. The avatar's globe becomes a two-tone disc with its wire cut out; its equator becomes a lilac orbit, one unbroken ring, thin behind the globe and heavy in front, where it passes over the letters. 1NT is drawn in heavy pixel capitals after the site's MS PGothic, centred on the globe, with a lilac stripe three cells deep across the letters.

  • Clear space: a quarter of the logo's width on every side (the hatched area).
  • Pixel art: the logo is set on a 64-cell grid, so it sits with the site's sprites; scale it by whole multiples where you can.
  • Smooth alternate: the same drawing in vector curves, for large prints and embroidery where the cells would be too coarse.
  • Animated: a looping GIF for streams and pages; the globe turns one meridian step, a glint runs round the orbit and the sparkles blink. Show the still logo to visitors who ask for reduced motion.
  • One colour: white on Night, or ink on lilac; the cut-outs carry the drawing.

Logo design: Macey Musto.

Palette

Black carries the shirt, white carries the names, grey carries the globe. Lilac is the team's colour and appears only as the equator, piping, tipping and trim. No dark purple, and no second accent.

07

Usage rules

Do

  • Keep the band level and unbroken; only the logo or number may sit over it, centred on it.
  • Rim every letter, so it holds on the globe lines.
  • Wear Night for every match; there is no change kit.
  • Match the headset or webcam frame to the band colour on stream.

Don't

  • Recolour the logo or put the globe on a dark purple.
  • Add a second accent colour; lilac is the only one.
  • Set names or numbers in any face but the pixel font, or leave them one pixel thin.
  • Add sponsor marks inside the band or over the logo.
