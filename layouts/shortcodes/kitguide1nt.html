ESEA League · North America · jersey branding guide

1NT Night, 2026

One jersey, Night, built from the team avatar: a black shirt with the wireframe globe printed across it, the glowing lilac equator as its band, and the crest as the chest mark. Every rule below exists so it reads as 1NT at stream resolution, 720p in a corner of a webcam frame.

01_mark{{ $win | safeHTML }}

01

Mark

The crest redraws the avatar at print resolution. The globe keeps its nine parallels and four meridian pairs, lit from the upper left; the equator stays the one coloured element. The letters are the avatar's own, cell for cell: tall two-pixel strokes with a light rim. On the jersey it is the chest mark, 35 cm across, its equator running straight into the shirt's band.

  • Clear space: a quarter of the crest's diameter on every side (the hatched area).
  • Minimum size: 40 mm on fabric, 24 px on screen. Below 24 px use the pixel avatar.
  • Patch: embroidered, with a satin-stitched border, so it stands off the print.

Palette

Black carries the shirt, white carries the names, grey carries the globe. Lilac is the team's colour and appears only as the equator, piping, tipping and trim. No dark purple, and no second accent.

Names and numbers are set in the site's pixel font (MS PGothic's 16 px bitmap, the DS-style face used across arkndjl.net), bolded to two-pixel strokes so they match the crest's tall 1NT letters. Grenze Gotisch stays for headings in print and in this guide.

Gamertagpixel font, bolded, tracking +2%, cap height 5 cm, centred on the spine 17 cm below the collar, at most 44 cm wide. Set as written, case and underscores kept.
Numberpixel font, bolded, 25 cm tall, centred on the spine with its middle on the equator band, the way the avatar's letters sit on its equator.
Rimwhite letters with an ink rim, heat-pressed: a soft gloss down each glyph and a shadow where the film stands off the cloth.
Small textsponsor and league lines in the site's pixel font, tracked wide.

07

Usage rules

Do

  • Keep the band level and unbroken; only the crest or number may sit over it, centred on it.
  • Rim every letter, so it holds on the globe lines.
  • Wear Night for every match; there is no change kit.
  • Match the headset or webcam frame to the band colour on stream.

Don't

  • Recolour the crest or put the globe on a dark purple.
  • Add a second accent colour; lilac is the only one.
  • Set names or numbers in any face but the pixel font, or leave them one pixel thin.
  • Add sponsor marks inside the band or over the crest.
