how a Major sticker is built reference

  1. die-cutThe sticker is cut around the art, not to a square, with a white peel border of even width.
  2. team stickerThe team's logo alone, at full size, with the event's name printed small under it on the border.
  3. signature stickerThe player's autograph written across a faded copy of the team logo; the cut follows both.
  4. event stampA small tab with team and event ties every signature to the tournament it came from.
