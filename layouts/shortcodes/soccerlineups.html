League matches this season (Understat lineups, big five). Shape = the starting positions read back to front (D, then the midfield bands DM / M / AM, then forwards), so wing-backs listed in midfield read as 3-4-x or 5-x-x by where Understat puts them. The XI is the most-used player at each spot of the most-used shape (starts at that spot in that shape). Changes = starters changed from the previous league match, on average. Pairs = outfield players with the most league minutes on the pitch together; xGD/90 = team xG for minus against a 90 while both were on (shot minutes; stoppage time folded into 90, so small samples swing). Rotation and lineup stability were tested as a game-model input: it helps only once the XI is announced, and the closing line already has it.
