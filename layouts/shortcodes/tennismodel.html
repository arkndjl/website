One overall rating and one per surface, blended {{ printf "%.0f" (mul (sub 1 $p.SW) 100) }} / {{ printf "%.0f" (mul $p.SW 100) }}; retirements count x{{ $p.RET }}.
  • Games won (x{{ printf "%.2f" (float $p.G) }}): a rating in log-odds of winning a game, moved by the share of games won against the opponent's rating ({{ printf "%.0f" (mul $p.GSW 100) }}% surface-specific). It reads a 6-1 6-1 win as more than a 7-6 7-6 one.
  • Serve and return points (x{{ printf "%.2f" (float $p.B) }}): opponent-adjusted serve and return strength from points won on serve, a Kalman filter per player (new players and long layoffs move faster), run through the game, tiebreak, set and match chain for best of three or five. It also gives the SPW+ / RPW+ columns.
