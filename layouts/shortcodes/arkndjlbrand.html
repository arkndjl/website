arkndjl :: brand and asset guide

brand

The rules every page, sprite, card and banner on arkndjl.net follows. Clean, cybergothic, terminal-native: purple ground, lilac structure, aquamarine voice.

01mark

The mark is the alchemical glyph for philosophers' sulfur (U+1F70E), drawn from Noto Sans Symbols into one vector outline. It is the only logo: the header, the favicon, the share cards, the uniform crests.

{{- range (slice (slice "#4a3b6b" "#c8c7ff" "lilac on purple" "default, the site header") (slice "#c8c7ff" "#4a3b6b" "purple on lilac" "avatar, favicon tile") (slice "#241a3a" "#7fffd4" "aquamarine on dark" "secondary") (slice "#131316" "#c8c7ff" "lilac on black" "ARKNDJL art, kits")) }} {{- end }}
  • Clear space: a quarter of the emblem's diameter on every side.
  • Minimum size: glyph 16 px, emblem 48 px, wordmark 24 px; 5 px tall in pixel art.
  • One flat colour from the palette. Lilac on purple by default.
  • Never stretch, rotate, outline, add a gradient or glow, or draw it as an emoji.

07gfx and components

window.exe__□×
Classic-Windows panels hold every list, table and board: bevel, violet title bar, status bar.
  • Plain glyphs for controls and arrows: ← → ▾ × □ :: ·. Pixel art for anything pictorial.
  • Pixel art scales by whole numbers with crisp edges (image-rendering: pixelated, shape-rendering: crispEdges).
  • Team logos stay in full colour and are never pixelated or recoloured.
  • Every layout works at phone width: 16 px gutters, 44 px tap targets, no sideways page scroll, the label column pinned on wide tables.
  • Generated text reads like a scouting report: plain, specific, no hype. Personal copy is brief, unemotional and never boasts.
  • No emoji anywhere: not in copy, buttons, cards or generated text.
  • No drop shadows or blurred glows. A hard one-step offset is the only shadow.

08ARKNDJL pages

  • Each piece of ARKNDJL work gets its own page at /arkndjl/<name>/ with its own design, built for what it shows. It is linked from the hub, the ARKNDJL menu and its group page.
  • Current pages: the hub (low-poly reliquary), music (y2k disc), art (neo-gothic nave), uniforms (PS1 select screen), 1NT kit (broadcast sheet), and this guide (spec sheet).
  • The art pages draw from y2k, neo-gothic, cyber transcendentalism and low-poly, in lilac and aquamarine over black.
