how it works: DraftKings' lines through ESPN every 30 minutes. a rule looks at a game once, at the first line we see with our model's number beside it (within 6 hours of the first line), and bets that line at that price with the model as it stood then. stake = Dengler Units, the Predictions picks' stake: Kelly on the edge our calibration says the bet is worth at the close (the backtest's line move per point of gap), shrunk for how sure that edge is, 1 DU = 1% of the bankroll now; the market's DU cap a game (rules on one game share it), {{ $d.stakeRules.slateCap }}% open on a slate. bets logged before the change (Oct 21 2026) kept their old quarter-Kelly stakes, and every rule's clock restarted with it. kill switch: a rule whose last {{ $d.kill.window }} graded bets ({{ $d.kill.minN }}+) are worth t below {{ $d.kill.t }} at the close stops (logged at 0 DU) until it is changed. the backtest column is the history's opening line at -110, an expectation, not part of the record; NBA's is an upper bound (the old model saw tip-time injury news).
