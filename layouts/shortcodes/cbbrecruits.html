Target. The NBA role a recruit's career reached, on the CBB board's FV scale (scripts/cbb_link.py): 20 never reached the NBA, 30 reached it, 35 100+ games, 40 3+ rotation seasons, 45 2+ seasons as a starter, 50 4+, 55 6+ or an All-Star, 60 2+ All-Star, 65-80 All-NBA. The headline is the expected FV, the mean outcome.

Sample. Every recruit On3's industry rankings list in the {{ index $m.train.classes 0 }}-{{ index $m.train.classes 1 }} classes ({{ $m.train.n }}), including the ones who went pro without college (overseas, prep to pro). {{ $m.train.nba }} reached the NBA. Each is linked to his NBA career through his college career, else by name with a class that fits his NBA age.

Model. Two parts, as the board's: the chance he reaches the NBA (logistic) and his FV if he does (log of FV above 20, mean by the residuals' own spread). Drivers are kept only if they help with whole classes held out, in at least 6 of 8 classes at t 2 or better. Kept for the chance: {{ delimit $m.kept.p ", " }}. Kept for the FV if he gets there: {{ delimit $m.kept.value ", " }}.

Held out. Rank correlation with the realized FV, each class predicted by a model fit on the others: consensus rank alone {{ printf "%.3f" $m.heldout.rank }}, model {{ printf "%.3f" $m.heldout.model }} (t {{ $m.heldout.t }}, better in {{ $m.heldout.wins }} of 8 classes). Forward, on careers still running: 2017 {{ (index $m.forward "2017").rank }} to {{ (index $m.forward "2017").model }}, 2018 {{ (index $m.forward "2018").rank }} to {{ (index $m.forward "2018").model }}.

ARK stars. Our 1-5 stars cut a recruit's FV at outcome bands: 5 = 2+ seasons as an NBA starter (FV 45+), 4 = reached the NBA, 3 = a D-I season as a starter (60%+ of minutes) at BPM +3 or better, 2 = played D-I, 1 = did not. Cuts are fit to the bands (weighted kappa) with whole classes held out; the industry's star shares are the other rule tried. R² of realized FV on the star: {{ printf "%.1f" (mul $pk.r2 100) }}% (bands), {{ printf "%.1f" (mul $sh.r2 100) }}% (industry shares), {{ printf "%.1f" (mul $ind.r2 100) }}% (industry stars); rank correlation {{ printf "%.3f" $pk.rho }}, {{ printf "%.3f" $sh.rho }}, {{ printf "%.3f" $ind.rho }}.
