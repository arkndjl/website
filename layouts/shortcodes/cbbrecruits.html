Target. What a recruit becomes in the NBA, valued as a share of a team. Each NBA season is VORX (XPM, the 50/50 BPM and DPM blend, above a replacement level of {{ index .scale.vorx 1 }}, times minutes) as a share of that season's average team. Peak = the mean of his best three seasons at ages {{ index .scale.ages 0 }}-{{ index .scale.ages 1 }}; a season he did not play counts 0. FV maps the share to 20-80 the same way on every ARKBOARD: 20 = replacement, 50 = 5% of a team, 80 = 80%. The headline is FV of the expected share, so it is the mean outcome.

Sample. Every recruit On3's industry rankings list in the {{ index $m.train.classes 0 }}-{{ index $m.train.classes 1 }} classes ({{ $m.train.n }}), including the ones who went pro without college (G League Ignite, Overtime Elite, overseas). {{ $m.train.nba }} added NBA value. Each is linked to his NBA career through his college career, else by name with a class that fits his NBA age.

Model. Two parts: the chance he adds NBA value (logistic), and how much if he does (log share, with its own residual spread). Drivers are kept only if they help with whole classes held out, in at least 6 of 9 classes at t 2 or better. Kept for the chance: {{ delimit $m.kept.p ", " }}. Kept for the size: {{ delimit $m.kept.value ", " }}.

Held out.
