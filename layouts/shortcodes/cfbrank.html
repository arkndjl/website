ARK rankings :: how the inputs are weighted_ _□×

Our score is the margin a team would be expected to win by against an average FBS team on a neutral field. The weights come from the game model's walk-forward backtest: every week of {{ $ev.trainSeason }}{{ with $ev.testSeason }} and {{ . }}{{ end }} is predicted only from the weeks before it, and a regression of the final margins on those predictions decides how much each input counts. So an input earns weight by predicting games that hadn't been played yet, not by agreeing with the polls.
