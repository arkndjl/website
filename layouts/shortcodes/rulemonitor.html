how it works: each rule's clock starts when it is switched on. with {{ $p.minN }}+ bets judged by the close, a rule stops if its last {{ $p.window }} are worth t below {{ $p.t }} at the close (clustered by game day), or if its CUSUM crosses {{ $p.cusum.h }}: each bet adds its shortfall against the close in units of the backtest's spread (sd), less {{ $p.cusum.k }}, and the sum never drops below 0, so it climbs only while the rule loses to the close and fires about {{ $p.cusum.h }} sd of losses in. a rule losing 1.5% a bet stops in about 60 bets, one that turns from +3% to -3% about 100 bets after the turn, and one really worth +3% almost never. a stopped rule makes no picks and its open ones are withdrawn; the paper book logs it at 0 DU. it comes back only when it is refit, which restarts its clock. the thresholds were registered on {{ lower ((time $p.registered).Format "Jan 2 2006") }}, before any of these bets were judged.
