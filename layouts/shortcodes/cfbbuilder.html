Budget. budget = program NIL budget (yNIL paid this season; NIL block anchors it to revenue-share cap + outside money) x 1.04 (cap growth). class reserve = this year's first-year players' yNIL x 1.04. retention order: value per dollar = edge (room WAR a season with him - without him, college WAR by FV) / (xNIL x P(return)). true budget ~ normal(estimate, SD 25% of estimate) (assumption; no program's figure is public). cost of a dollar = P(past true budget); a dollar surely over must buy its market worth (WAR at the level's $ per WAR). keep returner / sign target while value per market dollar > that cost; rest = can't keep at market (P(stay) capped at 25%). portal space = budget - reserve - retention; plans may exceed it. let walk: returner at $150K+, edge <= 0.14 WAR (x P(return)), at most 2 a team; released when his cost (xNIL x P(return)) signs a target adding >= 2x his edge, net >= minimum signing gain.

Rooms. next season's starters: 400 simulated offseasons (each player present with P(stay); hole = level's typical first backup). contender bar = 67th percentile of this season's units at the level. targets: players elsewhere with Transfer% >= 8% (FCS move-up odds >= 5%) who beat the room's last snap-playing spot by 2+ FV; price = level's going rate at position and FV, >= current xNIL.
