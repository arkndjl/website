ARKNDJLATHLETICS

PRESS START

One club, five sports, four kits each. Every uniform is cut to the same athlete, about 6'2" at 7.5 heads tall, so a helmet, a sleeve and a sock all sit where they would on a real player. Lilac is home, grey is the road kit, black with lilac is the alternate, and all-aquamarine is alternate 2. Black is the alternate.

01 mark{{ $win | safeHTML }}

  • The site glyph is the only logo. It is the hockey crest, the cap and hockey helmet, the football collar, the soccer badge, the hip and shorts mark, and the chain pendant.
  • It is one flat colour, with no gradient or shadow. A one-pixel ring in the trim colour is allowed when it sits on a busy field, as on the hockey crest.
  • It should be at least 5 px tall in pixel art. Below that it becomes a blob, so use the wordmark instead.
  • Never rotate, stretch or outline it in purple.

  • Each kit is about 60% base, 25% white or charcoal, and 15% accent.
  • Home is lilac with charcoal trim and an aquamarine accent. Road is grey with charcoal trim, aquamarine accents and lilac numbers. Alternate is black with lilac trim and lettering and an aquamarine accent. Alternate 2 is all aquamarine with charcoal trim and a lilac accent.
  • No dark purple anywhere: not #57068c, #241a3a or #4a3b6b. Grey, white and black are the only neutrals.

03 lettering and numbers{{ $win | safeHTML }}

  • Wordmark: ARKNDJL in the 4x7 face for titles, or the 3x5 face across a chest. It is always all capitals with one pixel between letters, and always centred.
  • Numbers: 6x10 block numerals with a one-pixel ring: charcoal with a white ring on lilac and aquamarine, lilac with a charcoal ring on grey and black. Secondary numbers (TV numbers, sleeves, shorts, baseball chest) use the 3x5 face.
  • Placement: the football front has no wordmark, only a small glyph crest at the collar under the V, above the number. Basketball has the wordmark over the number. Hockey carries the crest on the front and numbers on both upper sleeves. Soccer and baseball have the wordmark across the chest. Every back has the wordmark over the number.
  • Assigned numbers: football 11, basketball 1, hockey 31, soccer 10, baseball 7.

04 anatomy{{ $win | safeHTML }}

The pieces are layered in dressing order, from the bottom up: athlete, socks, shoes, jersey and pants (tucked or untucked by sport), headwear, then gear. Football, basketball and baseball tuck the jersey in. Hockey and soccer wear it over the pants or shorts.

05 kits{{ $win | safeHTML }}

Home (lilac), road (grey), alternate (black and lilac) and alternate 2 (aquamarine). Each sport's four kits, front and back, are on the select sport screen.

06 gear and extras{{ $win | safeHTML }}

  • Football gloves follow the jersey: lilac at home, grey on the road, aquamarine on alternate 2, and lilac on the black alternate. Hockey gloves are the kit colour (black on the alternate) with charcoal cuffs (lilac on the alternate). Batting gloves are white, or lilac on the alternate.
  • Sticks and bats are black, or charcoal on the alternate, with white tape or a ring in the kit colour.
  • Protective gear (leg guard, elbow guard) is charcoal, banded in the kit colour. The knee sleeve is black, or grey on the alternate.
  • The arm sleeve goes on one arm only, the off arm. It is black, or aquamarine against the black alternate.
  • Jewellery is silver only: a chain with a glyph pendant on basketball and baseball. Gold never appears.

07 usage rules{{ $win | safeHTML }}

  • Pixel art is the master. Low-poly renders are always made from the pixel textures, never drawn separately.
  • Light comes from the top left. Every piece has a highlight rim, a dithered shadow band and a selective outline; logos and numbers stay flat.
  • Don't mix kits: no home jersey with road pants. The exceptions are white football and baseball pants on home and alternate 2.
  • Don't use emoji, drop shadows, gradients or purple. Team logos elsewhere on the site stay in full colour; only ARKNDJL's own mark follows these rules.
  • To change a kit, edit KITS in scripts/uniforms.py, rerun it and scripts/jersey_ps1.py, then check the images on a phone.
