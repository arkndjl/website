uniforms.exe :: ARKNDJL athletics_□×

One club, five sports, three kits each. Every uniform is cut to the same athlete, about 6'2" at 7.5 heads tall, so a helmet, a sleeve and a sock all sit where they would on a real player. Lilac is home, aquamarine is away, black is the alternate.

01 mark_□×
  • The site glyph is the only logo. It is the hockey crest, the cap and helmet decal, the soccer badge, the hip and shorts mark, and the chain pendant.
  • It is one flat colour, with no gradient or shadow. A one-pixel ring in the trim colour is allowed when it sits on a busy field, as on the hockey crest.
  • It should be at least 5 px tall in pixel art. Below that it becomes a blob, so use the wordmark instead.
  • Never rotate, stretch or outline it in purple.

  • Each kit is about 60% base, 25% white or charcoal, and 15% accent.
  • Home is lilac with charcoal trim and an aquamarine accent. Away is aquamarine with charcoal trim and a lilac accent. Alternate is black with aquamarine trim and lilac lettering.
  • No dark purple anywhere: not #57068c, #241a3a or #4a3b6b. Grey, white and black are the only neutrals.
03 lettering and numbers_□×
  • Wordmark: ARKNDJL in the 4x7 face for titles, or the 3x5 face across a chest. It is always all capitals with one pixel between letters, and always centred.
  • Numbers: 6x10 block numerals with a one-pixel ring: charcoal with a white ring on lilac and aquamarine, lilac with an aquamarine ring on black. Secondary numbers (TV numbers, shorts, baseball chest) use the 3x5 face.
  • Placement: on football and basketball the wordmark sits over the number on the front; on hockey the front carries the crest; soccer and baseball have the wordmark across the chest. Every back has the wordmark over the number.
  • Assigned numbers: football 11, basketball 1, hockey 31, soccer 10, baseball 7.
04 anatomy_□×

The pieces are layered in dressing order, from the bottom up: athlete, socks, shoes, jersey and pants (tucked or untucked by sport), headwear, then gear. Football, basketball and baseball tuck the jersey in. Hockey and soccer wear it over the pants or shorts.

06 gear and extras_□×
  • Football gloves follow the jersey: lilac at home, aquamarine away and on the alternate. Hockey gloves are the kit colour (black on the alternate) with charcoal cuffs (aquamarine on the alternate). Batting gloves are white, or aquamarine on the alternate.
  • Sticks and bats are black, or charcoal on the alternate, with white tape or a ring in the kit colour.
  • Protective gear (leg guard, elbow guard) is charcoal, banded in the kit colour. The knee sleeve is black, or grey on the alternate.
  • The arm sleeve goes on one arm only, the off arm. It is black, or lilac against the black alternate.
  • Jewellery is silver only: a chain with a glyph pendant on basketball and baseball. Gold never appears.
07 usage rules_□×
  • Pixel art is the master. Low-poly renders are always made from the pixel textures, never drawn separately.
  • Light comes from the top left. Every piece has a highlight rim, a dithered shadow band and a selective outline; logos and numbers stay flat.
  • Don't mix kits: no home jersey with away pants. The exceptions are white football pants on every kit except the alternate, and road grey for baseball away.
  • Don't use emoji, drop shadows, gradients or purple. Team logos elsewhere on the site stay in full colour; only ARKNDJL's own mark follows these rules.
  • To change a kit, edit KITS in scripts/uniforms.py, rerun it and scripts/jersey_ps1.py, then check the images on a phone.
