Offense personnel is backs then tight ends: 11 = 1 RB, 1 TE, 3 WR; 12 = 1 RB, 2 TE; 21 = 2 backs (fullbacks count), 1 TE; jumbo = six or more linemen; heavy = 12, 13, 21, 22 and jumbo. Defense: base = 4 or fewer defensive backs, nickel = 5, dime = 6+. Runs and dropbacks, regular season, no kneels or spikes. EPA a play and success rate (nflverse); ranks 1-32, best first (most used for usage, lowest for EPA allowed); numbers on fewer than 20 plays are left out.
