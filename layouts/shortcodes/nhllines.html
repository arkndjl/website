5-on-5 only (five skaters and a goalie each side), from the NHL's shift charts (its HTML time-on-ice reports when the API has none) and MoneyPuck's shot expected goals. Current lines are the last game's forward trios and defence pairs ordered by 5v5 time together in that game; the numbers beside them are the unit's season so far. GF% and xGF% are the unit's share of goals and expected goals while together. On/off: the team's xGF% with the skater on the ice and in his games with him off it; rel is the gap. Linemate: the skater he played the most 5v5 time with, their xGF% together, his without that linemate and the linemate's without him (60+ minutes apart). Small samples: under ~100 minutes these swing a lot.
