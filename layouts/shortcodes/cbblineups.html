{{ if eq $view "units" -}} Five-man units from NCAA play-by-play substitutions: who was on the floor between subs, the points each side scored, and the game's possessions (box-score count) shared by time on the floor. ORtg / DRtg = points scored / allowed per 100 possessions; Net = ORtg - DRtg. sNet shrinks Net toward the team's own net with 150 possessions, so a unit with a few minutes reads close to the team. Units with 2+ minutes, most-used first. {{- else -}} On = the team's net rating (points per 100 possessions, scored minus allowed) with the player on the floor; Off = with him on the bench; On-Off = the gap. sOn-Off shrinks the gap toward 0 by its noise (600 harmonic possessions on/off), so a bench player with a hot 80 minutes doesn't top the list. On/off measures the lineups a player shares, not the player alone: a starter who plays with the other starters inherits their gap. FV is the board's grade. All D-I lists players with 200+ minutes, by sOn-Off. {{- end }} Source: NCAA play-by-play; a game without substitution lines is left out (the count shows how many were read).
