Our metrics. Elo runs on every pro match since 1991 at every level (FiveThirtyEight's form: K = 250 / (matches + 5)0.4, Slams x1.1), carried past the archive's end on Tennis Abstract's results. Elo+ sets it against players the same age with 20+ pro matches, 100 + 25 a standard deviation. Game dominance (games won over what the Elo gap predicts, {{ cond (eq $tour "atp") "0.043" "0.053" }} of game share per 100 Elo) was built for this board and tested; it misses the gate on both tours, as Elo already holds most of what it knows. Badges (medals by the name) are traits that predicted the peak beyond the FV, birth years held out, t clustered by player (scripts/tennis_badge_backtest.py): {{ if eq $tour "wta" }}Server (serve points won over the last 52 weeks, regressed by volume; t 2.0, positive at all four snapshots) passes; aces, return points, game dominance, tiebreaks and upset wins do not{{ else }}none passes on the ATP side (aces, serve and return points won, game dominance, tiebreaks, upset wins){{ end }}; badges show, they do not move the FV. Height and "no listed height" never enter: the archive fills heights in mostly for players who went on to make it, so a missing height was worth {{ cond (eq $tour "atp") "3.4" "6.2" }} FV out of sample, which is hindsight, not scouting.
