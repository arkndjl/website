{{- else if eq $part "method" }}
method.txt_{{ $btns }}
  1. Win chance. Each five's mean FACEIT elo (weight .80) and team talent (.20) on a 600-point scale. Held out on 149 S59 league matches its log loss was .586, against .613 for the power ranking and .661 for the record.
  2. Veto. Each player's lifetime FACEIT win rate on the map against that player's overall rate, shrunk by n/(n+50), averaged over the five and scaled 1.18 on the match log-odds; held out t −3.96. Team map records and pistol share failed, so the map book above is for reading the past.
  3. Rounds. A race to 13 where the round-win chance varies within a map (logit-normal, τ .55). It matches the overtime rate and every total line within 2 points; strength gaps did not move totals held out, so the line is a flat 20.5.
  4. ARK rating. Per-round box-score stats, each regressed to the mean by its split-half reliability, fit to round win share (R² {{ with $d.arkModel }}{{ printf "%.2f" (float .r2) }}{{ end }}).
  5. Season odds. 10,000 simulations of the rest of the regular season on elo win chances; every scheduled match is played out, the rest at each team's mean chance.
