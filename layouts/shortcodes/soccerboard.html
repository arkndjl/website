Why minutes x club strength and not the Soccer page's WAR: on the big five 2015-26, a club's players' value per 90 last season, weighted by this season's minutes, explains 70% of the variance in this season's club rating out of sample with this value and 63% with WAR (both together 71%), and it holds steadier from season to season (.84 vs .69 for players who stay, .52 vs .47 for movers). Tested and not kept: market value change on the year, value two seasons ago, last season's minutes share, club rating and scoring rate, European minutes, matchday squads, Premier League 2 scoring, second-tier league, a move to a stronger club, age x minutes share, height for keepers.
