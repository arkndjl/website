What it projects. For each recruit: the chance he is on the field in a given year of college (on the board, with snaps, FBS or FCS), his FV if he is, and the expected FV of the two (a player off the field counts at the board floor, 20). Fit on {{ lang.FormatNumber 0 $f.n }} signees from ESPN's {{ index $f.classes 0 }}-{{ index $f.classes 1 }} classes, each followed to this season's board: the {{ index $f.classes 1 }} class shows year 1, the {{ index $f.classes 0 }} class year 4. {{ math.Round (mul $f.onRate 100) }}% of them are on the board.

Tested out of sample. Five folds that hold out whole programs. The board-or-not model ranks recruits with an AUC of {{ printf "%.3f" $t.auc }}; FV on the board is predicted to {{ printf "%.1f" $t.rmse_on }} FV RMSE (R² {{ printf "%.2f" $t.r2_on }}); the expected FV correlates {{ printf "%.2f" $t.r }} with what happened (R² {{ printf "%.2f" $t.r2 }}). Against simpler fits on the same folds: position and year only, r {{ printf "%.2f" $b.pos_year.r }}; grade and year only, r {{ printf "%.2f" $b.grade_year.r }}.

Redshirts. A player on a roster with no snaps is scored at his next year, recalibrated on last season's sitters: of {{ lang.FormatNumber 0 $rs.n }} recruits on a {{ sub $f.season 1 }} roster who played four games or fewer, {{ math.Round (mul $rs.rate 100) }}% are on the board this season (against {{ math.Round (mul $rs.playedRate 100) }}% of those who played). Tested class by class: {{ range $i, $x := $rs.test }}{{ if $i }}; {{ end }}{{ $x.class }} class AUC {{ $x.auc }}, log loss {{ $x.ll }} vs {{ $x.llRate }} for the base rate{{ end }}. {{ math.Round (mul $rs.away 100) }}% of the sitters who reached the board did it at another school, so next season's roster counts a redshirt at his chance of playing times {{ math.Round (mul (sub 1 $rs.away) 100) }}%, and a commit at {{ math.Round (mul (sub 1 $f.away1) 100) }}% (the share of first-year players still at the school they signed with). Young players with less than a full game's snaps at the position blend their board FV with the recruit projection by snaps (half and half at one game's worth); past that the board FV stands.

Limits: one season seen across four classes, so year effects and class effects are mixed. Players drafted after three seasons leave the year-4 group, which understates the best recruits there. Five-stars reach the field more often than predicted (the top row above); the four-star hinge that would bend the curve there didn't pass the test across all recruits.
