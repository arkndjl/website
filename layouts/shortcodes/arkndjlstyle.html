  • Text is aquamarine; anything you can click or that names a thing (links, column heads, tab labels) is lilac.

  • One face everywhere: MS PGothic, as its 16 px bitmap strike in static/fonts/mspgothic-pixel.woff2. Body, tables, code, cards, the ARKNDJL pages, generated images.
  • Sizes are whole multiples of 16 px (16, 32, 48, 64) so every pixel lands on the grid. Small labels may use 12 or 13 px in dense tables only.
  • Lowercase by default: titles, tabs, buttons, menu items. Uppercase for column heads and tracked labels (letter-spacing .125em to .25em), and for the names ARKBOARD, ARKLIBRARY, ARKNDJL, 1NT.
  • Weight is normal. Emphasis is colour (aquamarine against lilac), not bold or italic. Big numbers on cards may be doubled.
  • Numbers: tabular, fixed decimals per column (.312, 4.7, 61%), a real minus sign (−) for negatives.
  • Never load another font, including for code, headings or a single page.

03pixel art

Everything pictorial is pixel art: icons, sprites, buttons, frames, badges, hero art. Sprites come from brand/make-pixel-art.py and scripts/pixelart.py.

{{- range $sprites }}{{ $f := printf "partials/px/%s.html" . }}{{ if templates.Exists $f }}{{ end }}{{ end }}
  • Scale by whole numbers only (1x, 2x, 3x) with image-rendering: pixelated and shape-rendering: crispEdges.
  • Draw on the grid: 16 by 16 for icons, 8 by 8 for button glyphs, 2 px strokes, no anti-aliasing.
  • Animation is stepped (steps() timing), one frame at a time, a few frames. No easing, no tweened motion.
  • Flat fills from the palette. Shading is a second flat colour or a dither, never a gradient.
  • The one exception: real team logos on boards and cards stay full-colour images, never pixelated or recoloured.
  • No vector illustration, photos as decoration, rounded corners or drop shadows on site parts.

07copy and voice

The site reads like a scouting report: brief, plain and unemotional. State what the number is and what it rests on.

  • Short and factual. Where more has to be said, use a few full sentences, not a string of fragments.
  • Lead with the result, then the reason: the grade, then the two or three things that drive it.
  • Name the stat and the sample: "71st percentile in yards per route run, 312 routes".
  • Generated text follows a template per position and sport, so every report reads the same way.
  • Use the site's own terms as defined in Reference: FV, xNIL, WAR, Stat+, provisional.
  • No marketing, hype, bragging, jokes, flavour text or exclamation marks.
  • No LLM phrasing: "dive into", "elevate", "game-changer", "it's worth noting", "not just X but Y".
  • No emoji anywhere: copy, buttons, cards, alerts, generated text.

do scouting report

55 FV. Above-average arm talent and pocket movement; 8.9 yards per attempt on 241 dropbacks. Sacks on 9% of pressures. Grade is provisional until a second season.

don't hype

This kid is an absolute game-changer! He elevates everyone around him and is primed for a breakout season you won't want to miss.

08layout

Mobile first. Design every page at phone width, then let it widen.

  • 16 px side gutters, 44 px tap targets, no sideways page scroll. Wide tables scroll inside their window with the label column pinned.
  • Spacing on an 8 px step (8, 16, 24, 48). The page grid is 48 px.
  • One idea per window. Group related windows under tabs, not one long page.
  • Each ARKNDJL piece gets its own page at /arkndjl/<name>/, designed for what it shows and linked from the hub.

do colour

Lilac column heads, aquamarine values, pink for the one number that fell.

don't colour

A new orange for a single chart, a soft glow behind a title, red and green arrows.

do icons and art

A 16 px pixel icon from the set, at 2x, beside its label.

don't icons and art

An emoji, a smooth vector clip-art icon, a pixel sprite scaled to 1.5x and blurred.

09mark

The alchemical glyph for philosophers' sulfur (U+1F70E) is the only logo: header, favicon and share cards.

python3 brand/make-glyph.py        # glyph, favicon.svg
node brand/render.js               # share card, favicons, banners
node brand/render-og.js            # one share card per ARKBOARD page
