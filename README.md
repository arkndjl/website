# arkndjl.net

Personal site of [arkndjl](https://arkndjl.net): sabermetrics, prospect evaluation, economics, art and music. Built with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io) and the [Terminal](https://github.com/panr/hugo-theme-terminal) theme, deployed to Cloudflare, which runs Hugo at build time and serves the generated `public/` folder (see `wrangler.toml`). `public/` is build output and is not tracked in git.

## College Football ARKBOARD (automated)

`data/cfb.json` is the board, built by `scripts/fetch_cfb.mjs` from PFN's impact rankings, On3's college grades and ESPN rosters, then graded by the 20-80 FV model in the same script. The `{{< cfbboard >}}` shortcode renders it on the ARKBOARD page, with filters and sortable columns.

```sh
npm i -D playwright && npx playwright install chromium   # once (On3 is behind Cloudflare)
node scripts/fetch_cfb.mjs             # fetch everything, regrade, rewrite data/cfb.json
node scripts/fetch_cfb.mjs --offline   # regrade from the cached pulls (scripts/.cache/cfb/), e.g. after tuning the model
node scripts/fetch_cfb.mjs --skip-on3  # refetch PFN + ESPN only
```

The board rules (`RULES`) and every model constant (`FV_*`, `STARTERS`) are at the top of the script. Hand overrides (dates of birth, FV grades, notes) go in `data/cfb_manual.json`. `.github/workflows/cfb-board.yml` runs the script Mondays and Wednesdays from August through January and commits the result, which triggers the Cloudflare build. It can also be run by hand from the Actions tab. Team logos are saved full-colour at 256 px into `static/img/cfb/logos/` (NFL: `static/img/nfl/`) when ImageMagick is installed.

The College Football tab has fourteen views: **all FBS** (every graded player, `static/data/cfb-all.json`, loaded on demand, 50 to a page), **draft board** (the same, limited to players eligible for the 2027 draft), **WAR leaders** (the same, ranked by college WAR from `scripts/cfb_av.mjs` (over replacement starters 2 SD below the average one, so team WAR sums to wins over a replacement team; NFL WAR uses the same anchor, fit by `scripts/nfl_war.py --calibrate`), FCS players against FCS starters, kickers and punters against an average one; opens on FBS), **games** (`data/cfb_games.json`: the next unplayed week, with Elo win probabilities and spreads, playstyle from CFBD advanced stats, injuries from ESPN, and each side's top five prospects on offense and defense), **projections** (`data/cfb_proj.json`: the week's top 300 offensive players by projected PPR points, from the same opponent-scaled per-game lines), **game table** (the same week as one sortable table: model spread vs the market line and the edge between them, and each game's projected top three on offense and defense from this season's per-game CFBD stat lines scaled by the opponent), **mock draft** (`data/cfb_mock.json`: a projection of how the draft will go, not our board: the 2027 order from ESPN's NFL standings, needs from each team's whole position room (ESPN rosters for age, salary and contract years, nflverse rosters for draft slot: each player's quality through next season is the better of his draft capital on a rookie deal and his pay against the top of the position's market, cut for age and an expiring deal; a group's need is half 1 minus its starters' quality and half its rank among the 32 rooms, and each pick fills part of it), projected trades in rounds 1-2 (⇅: a team up to 20 slots back moves up when a player is worth more to it, as a slot on Massey and Thaler's market pick-value curve, than its best option at its own pick, paying its pick plus the smallest later picks that cover the chart gap plus 5%; the team on the clock accepts when the extra beats what it loses moving back; at most one trade-up per team, ~3-5 in the first round; shown as a dashed ⇅ tag on the table and the GFX; the GFX has a round picker that draws a whole round, and lists each trade in it under the table: picks each way with their chart values and the player taken), picks already traded before the draft (Wikipedia's list of trades for the draft, `fetchNflTrades` / `applyNflTrades`: each pick moves to the team that holds it, a solid "via" tag naming its original team, with the deal in its report), and an expected pick per prospect, a geometric blend of his consensus-board rank and his rank by our draft value, each mapped to a draft slot by the board-to-pick curve backtested on 2020-25 final boards (`scripts/draft_backtest.py`, `data/cfb_draft_backtest.json`: position, class, conference level and size added nothing out of sample once a board ranks a player; a missing combine invite and the 40 time did, and apply once the draft year's combine is published) (FV plus positional value and the per-round position counts in `data/cfb_draft_priors.json`, frame, and position-change flags at the new spot); three mocks share the order, needs and trade rules: **predictive** (`data/cfb_mock_pred.json`, the expected pick above: blending the model's order into the board cut slot error from .452 to .433 on the rebuilt 2020-23 boards, best at a board weight of .75-.85, `scripts/cfb_fv_refit.mjs --mock`), **blended** (`data/cfb_mock.json`: each slot halfway between predictive and conviction on the log scale) and **conviction**; the **conviction mock** (`data/cfb_mock_conv.json`) runs the same order, needs and trade rules on the model's own board alone: FV before the consensus blend, no market positional premium or price, no position-run penalty and no combine terms, so each team takes the best player for it by our grade and fit, and trades up when its target would go first on that board; predictive and conviction grade each other and both grade the blended mock: a pick the grading mock makes 2.5x+ and 16+ picks later is a **reach**, one it makes that much earlier a **value** (site table only; `mockDrafts`); a curve refit on fall boards (`scripts/mock_fall_curve_backtest.py`, `data/cfb_mock_fall_backtest.json`) won 3 of 5 drafts and lost both NIL-era ones, so the predictive mock keeps the final-board curve; clicking a pick opens its report: need, slot against projection, consensus and our board, value, position change, type and badges, and the player's strengths and concerns, all templated from the numbers in `mockDraft`), **team talent** and **staff** (`data/cfb_teams.json`: projected starting-22 talent, Elo with a preseason prior from last season's Elo, SP+ and recruiting, TvE, team xNIL, and head coach / coordinator credit). NIL comes in three numbers, all priced off On3's valuations: **yNIL** (year NIL) is what a player was likely paid going into the season, his share of his program's budget (anchored to the revenue-share cap: ~$20M at Power 4 schools, ~$3M in the Group of Five, `XNIL`) from what was known before the season (earlier seasons' production, On3 grade, recruiting, position); **xNIL** (expected NIL) is what he's worth on the open market now, from this season's FV, position and role (the on-field value is expected AV at his FV or this season's college AV, priced per college WAR in `nilFit.perWar`, `p.sav`, whichever fits On3's valuations better that run: `nilFit.value` and `nilFit.valueTest` in `data/cfb.json`), priced on a Power 4 or Group of Five curve (a G5 player good enough to start at P4 is priced in the P4 market); FCS players are priced off the FBS fit: their programs split ~$0.4M (FCS schools mostly have no revenue sharing), and an FCS player good enough to start in the G5 or P4 is priced on that curve at 40% (`XNIL.fcsMove`: he only collects it by transferring up); **zNIL** = xNIL − yNIL, the value gained this season, summed for teams (team talent tab) and coaches (staff tab: the roster for a head coach, the side for a coordinator). On the staff tab zNIL is a fifth of each coach's score: growth over what the roster was paid, log((xNIL + $250K) / (yNIL + $250K)), standardized within the role and the coach's tier (P4 / G5, since a G5 roster's yNIL comes from a small budget) and capped at 2.5 SD; the rest is Elo and TvE for head coaches, production over talent and development for coordinators. The **fbs fv to xNIL** and **fcs fv to xNIL** windows show each FV tier's xNIL range in each division; the FCS one adds the FCS discount at equal FV (FCS median / FBS median per tier, and overall as the FCS-player-weighted geometric mean, `fcs.nilDiscount` in `data/cfb.json`). For draft-eligible prospects, FV is then blended toward the grade implied by PFN's industry consensus big board (seven analyst boards averaged), up to 35% with all seven boards (`CONS_W`), because consensus rank was the strongest single predictor of NFL outcome in the backtest. `scripts/cfb_teams.mjs` holds the team, games, staff and mock models. Coordinators come from `data/cfb_staff.json` when present. Recruiting ratings, SP+ and advanced stats come from the CollegeFootballData API, which needs a free key in the `CFBD_API_KEY` environment variable (a repository secret for the workflow); the pulls are cached in `scripts/.cache/cfb/`. Game projections come from `scripts/cfb_model.mjs`. It rates teams with Elo plus opponent-adjusted EPA per play (garbage time removed). The ratings are solved as a ridge regression shrunk toward a preseason prior: last season's Elo, SP+ and efficiency, plus this season's 247 talent composite and returning production, weighted by a regression fit one season ahead. On top of the ratings it adds home field, rest, time zones, travel distance and altitude. The blend weights and settings (Elo K, Elo home field, prior strength) are fit on walk-forward predictions, where each week is rated only on earlier weeks. Fitting uses last season; this season is scored out of sample against the closing line (log loss, Brier, MAE, ATS by edge size). The **model** tab shows that report and the **season odds** tab shows 10,000 simulated seasons (projected wins, conference title game and title, playoff and bowl odds); both are in `data/cfb_model.json`. Win, cover and over chances are normal around the model margin and total. Totals are plays × points per play plus wind, rain and cold (Open-Meteo, cached by game in `weather.json`). A starting QB listed out is replaced by his backup's EPA per play. Every coming game's model line is logged to `data/cfb_edges.json` and graded against the closing line once played. **Availability** (`data/cfb_injuries.json`): Rotoballer's college news (the last ~5 days, each headline clause read for a status) and ESPN's report, matched to rosters; the newest item per player wins, short-term statuses lapse after 9 days, season-ending ones stand until news says otherwise, and `injuries` in `data/cfb_manual.json` overrides both. Statuses carry a chance of playing (out 0, doubtful .25, questionable .6, probable .9) that scales each starter's cost in the projections; a season-ending injury takes the player off the projected depth chart and costs 1 FV (2 for an ACL/Achilles). **ARKLIST** (`data/arklist.json`, one hand-kept list per sport) marks favourites with the site glyph and fills each sport page's ARKLIST tab; `/arkboard/arklist/` shows every sport's list together. Entries can be prospects (2027-29 recruits, internationals) and coaches; `layouts/shortcodes/arklist.html` matches them against the boards in the browser. **Consensus** adds Tankathon's and PFF's big boards to PFN's seven analyst boards (nine in all); a **riser** (↗) is a draft-eligible prospect the model grades 6+ FV above his consensus rank's grade, or a 55+ no board ranks. **FV** also weighs age on a curve (outliers move most), class runway, a first-year bonus at positions where young contributors are rare (measured each run), frame projected to age 22 along each position's age-weight slope, snap share, and ESPN's schedule-adjusted QBR for quarterbacks. At OL, CB and S the PFN efficiency stats FV used to weigh on trust (pressure rate and sacks allowed, forced incompletions, catch rate and rating allowed, tackle rate) were tested once PFN had two seasons there: against the 2025 and 2026 drafts, holding production fixed, none added anything (`scripts/cfb_olsdb_backtest.mjs`, `data/cfb_olsdb_backtest.json`), so they carry no weight, as at the other positions. **Position changes** (⇄, the position-change filter) flag a player whose weight at 22 and production fit another NFL spot. Every path is backtested on the 2020-26 combines (OL and the secondary 2025-26, PFN's first seasons there) against where the NFL used each player (nflverse depth charts, else the combine listing; `scripts/cfb_move_backtest.mjs`, `data/cfb_move_backtest.json`). The line between a DT and an end is the lightest NFL DTs' weight (10th pct, ~282 lb) in both directions. Light receiving tight ends, small receiving backs, undersized linebackers, tall corners and long guards were never moved, so those flags are gone. 23 of 35 flags were right, against 13 of 53 before. **Badges** (`scripts/cfb_badges.mjs`) are bronze-to-platinum trait badges (Blocker, Pass Rusher, Ballhawk, Route Runner…): each badge is one stat on the PFN line, regressed toward the position mean by volume (v' = m + (v − m) · n / (n + k), with k the volume at which the stat is half signal, from its year-to-year correlation in PFN's 2019-25 lines), then ranked among the position's 100+ snap players. Bronze is the 75th percentile (top 25%), Silver the 90th (top 10%), Gold the 97th (top 3%), Platinum the 99th (top 1%). Stats that barely repeat (QB INT rate, LB coverage, single coverage stats for DBs) are left out; corners and safeties get a coverage composite instead. **Archetypes** are a few general shapes per position built from the badges: each lists badge groups, scores the tier points (1-4) of the badges it covers, needs a badge in every group, and the top score of 2+ wins (two-group shapes like dual-threat TE or three-down end win when both halves are there); anyone short of that gets the position's base archetype. The badge and tier filters and the medals after the position use them. **Running back roles** (Every Down Back, Early Down Back, 3rd Down Back; the RB role filter and the chip after RB) come from estimated snap shares on standard and passing downs (passing downs: 2nd and 8+, 3rd or 4th and 5+): a back's snap share split by how his backfield share of carries + targets tilts between the two (`rbRoles` in `scripts/cfb_pbp.mjs`). Every-down is 50%+ of the snaps on both, early-down 30%+ on standard downs and 10+ points fewer on passing downs, 3rd-down the mirror; anyone else is a committee back. Calibrated on the 2024 NFL season, where participation data has the true snaps by down: the labels match the true ones for 82% of backs (81% six weeks in) and never mix up early-down and 3rd-down backs. **Kickers and punters** are graded from each team's field goals over expected and punt field position (CFBD drives) credited by share of attempts; a primary kicker's absence prices into the game projections. The **games** tab polls ESPN's scoreboard for live scores while games are on, and **top performers** lists the latest week's top three at each position from CFBD box scores. The workflow runs Sunday through Wednesday, Friday and Saturday. **Play-by-play** (`scripts/cfb_pbp.mjs`): CFBD plays, one call a week, parsed for ball carrier, target, sacker and hurrier, give each player EPA per carry, explosive rates and red-zone production; a stat enters FV only when it holds up in a split-half test (odd vs even games) this season, weighted by that reliability. The existing FV stats are reweighted the same way by their season-to-season correlation in PFN's history (pressure rate repeats at ~.5, sacks per pressure at ~0, so sack conversion is shown only as a regression flag). The age curve was backtested on the 2005-18 drafts (career AV vs the pick's expectation): youth is linear, the old tail steeper. Game-model inputs are kept only when they improve log loss in both the fit and the test season: opponent-adjusted success rate made it; explosiveness and red-zone points per trip did not. **Breakouts** (`data/cfb_breakout.json`) lists first- and second-year skill players whose touches and targets jumped over their last two games; **portal** (`data/cfb_portal.json`) lists this season's transfers with FV and each team's net expected NFL value in minus out; the **staff** tab adds scheme fingerprints (4th-down go rate, tempo, run rate, havoc, stuff rate, explosiveness allowed, as FBS percentiles). **Draft%** (`draftOdds` in `scripts/cfb_teams.mjs`) is the chance a draft-eligible player is picked in the next draft: the odds he declares (by class and projected slot) times the odds he's taken, with a spread that narrows as the draft nears, all fit on PFF's dated big boards for the 2020-26 drafts (`scripts/cfb_draft_pct_backtest.py`, `data/cfb_draft_pct_backtest.json`). **FBS%** on the FCS tab is an FCS player's chance of being on an FBS roster next season, fit on the 2023-26 FCS-to-FBS moves from ESPN rosters and stat lines (`scripts/cfb_fcs_up_fit.py`, `data/cfb_fcs_up_fit.json`; it also writes `data/cfb_fcs_fifth.json`, the FCS players in their 5th+ year, rerun each August). **Transfer%** (`scripts/cfb_transfer.mjs`, fit by `scripts/cfb_transfer_fit.py` into `data/cfb_transfer_fit.json`; rerun with `python3 scripts/cfb_transfer_fit.py`, or `--refit` from its cache in `scripts/.cache/transfer/`) is the chance a player is on another FBS or FCS roster next season: a ridge logit on every 2024 and 2025 FBS/FCS player followed to the next season's ESPN rosters (class, recruiting rank, playing time and production, a G5 producer's pull upward, team record, head-coach change odds, level, distance from home and home-state school, a transfer who just arrived, position), tested out of time on the 2025→26 cycle (AUC .736). Playing time is games with a stat line over the team's games (ESPN in the fit, CFBD box scores live): a P4 spot player (1-7 games) in his third year or later moves more than a regular, while a G5 or FCS regular moves more (bigger programs recruit him); adding it lifted AUC from .716, and PFN depth rank, volume share and the young players ahead of him added nothing. Program talent (the roster's mean recruiting grade, as a z-score) times production keeps a loaded program's starters home (8.5% of top-quarter producers at programs 1+ SD above average moved in 2024-25, over a third after a coach change), and a WR or TE whose starting QB transfers is likelier to go too (scored live as that QB's Transfer%); with both, AUC .738; an exit model shares the features and Draft% sets the floor on leaving. Hometowns are ESPN birthplaces geocoded with the Census Gazetteer (`scripts/geo/us_places.json`), schools are ESPN venues (`scripts/geo/team_loc.json`). It is a column on every board tab and a line in the scouting report. The **team builder** tab (`static/data/cfb-builder.json`, `teamBuilder` in the same module) plans each FBS program's offseason: budget = its yNIL sum up 4%, less the next recruiting class (this year's first-year players' yNIL) and the roster kept at market price × stay odds in order of value per dollar; next season's units from Monte Carlo returns against the level's 67th-percentile unit; targets are likely movers and FCS move-ups who'd start, priced at the level's going rate for their position and FV; targets are ranked by need-weighted WAR per dollar (college WAR = AV at FV × .095) × Transfer% × landing odds, the chance he picks this school if he moves, from a destination-choice model fit on 2024-25 transfers (`scripts/cfb_landing_fit.py`: level step, program talent vs his current school's, open playing time at his position, distance from home), so lower-tier programs lean on FCS move-ups (priced at 60% of the going rate) and players dropping a level; a round-robin market (at most four plans per target) signs the best value that fits; open starting spots with no likely returner push a team's mode up a step. Entrenched starters (top-quarter producers at such programs, with a steady head coach) are not portal targets, and a receiver's landing odds rise 7.2x (TE 5.7x) at a school whose plan signs his quarterback (`QB_LINK`, from 2024-25 co-moves). A program can also let a returner walk to free money (`WALK_*`): when his expected cost (xNIL × chance to return) signs a realistic target worth at least twice his edge over the next man up, for a net gain, he goes on the let-walk list (at most two a team). Head-coach change odds come from the coaching market (`data/cfb_coach_market.json`, its last run) where it has the team, and a target whose head coach could take the job gets that pull on his landing odds: in the 2025-26 cycle, 39% of transfers from programs whose head coach took another FBS job went with him (24x chance; `HC_FOLLOW`). First- and second-year players are kept and released on their projected next-season FV (the recruit projection's blend, else what their recruiting grade projects to), and a first-year player, or a second-year one projecting to 40+, is never let walk. Its **GFX plan** button draws the plan as a 4:5 card (the field runs the card's full width so every name fits in full; each starter's tile has his backup under it, the next player on next season's chart who is 50%+ to be there, as on the page's field; tiles are filled in a ten-step FV ramp: purples under 45 with a step at 25, teals 45-59, white at 60, gold at 70), and the hero sprite is `hero-builder` in `brand/make-pixel-art.py`. **Coaching market** (`scripts/cfb_coach.mjs`, fit by `scripts/cfb_coach_fit.py` into `data/cfb_coach_fit.json` with history in `data/cfb_coach_pay.json`; rerun with `python3 scripts/cfb_coach_fit.py`, or `--offline` from its cache in `scripts/.cache/coach/`) prices every head coach and coordinator and projects the coaching carousel. Pay is USA Today's salary database (head coaches 2006-25, assistants 2009-24, public schools; private schools report none). **Value** is log(pay) fit on the staff board's inputs and the program's, ridge, an input kept only if it cuts leave-one-out error by 1%+: for head coaches recruiting level, P4 and results (win% over three seasons and this one, plus pass rate over expected), LOO R² .87; for coordinators the program's head-coach pay, side and co-coordinator, LOO R² .75. Elo, TvE, zNIL, the staff score and 4th-down aggression add nothing to pay once those are in: the market doesn't price them, which is where value and pay part. **Openings**: a multinomial logit on 2010-25 school-seasons (same coach / hired away by another FBS program / fired or out) on this season's win% projected to the end (the season simulation), win% against the program's 10-year norm, a first or second year in the job and a Group of Five winner's pull upward; fit on 2010-19, tested on 2020-25 (log loss 16% under the base rate, AUC .72). **Hires**: a P4 opening went to a sitting FBS head coach 42% of the time, a coordinator (one of a staff's two best-paid assistants) 25%, someone else 33% (G5: 9 / 32 / 59); within each pool a conditional logit on 2008-25 hires picks the likeliest (head coaches: pay step up, results, level step, conference, distance; coordinators: pay, school's results, level, internal promotion; top-10 hit rate out of sample 29% and 38% against 8% and 5% by chance). **Staff**: when the head coach stays, 72% of coordinators are back; when he is hired away, 43% follow him; when he is fired, 9% stay. Each coach's Leave% and destinations come from those; the staff tab shows value and Leave%, the ▸ detail adds pay, surplus, buyout and where he'd go, and the **coaching jobs** and **coach pay** tabs list every job's odds with its likely hires and every public salary against value. `data/cfb_coach_market.json` holds the jobs and every projected move (`flows`: from, to, role, chance), for the team builder (players following a coach). FV constants are calibrated against NFL outcomes in `data/cfb_calibration.json` (nflverse drafts 2010-19: tier counts per position, composite reliability, size norms, per-round draft counts); the comments next to each constant give the numbers.

Any markdown table window can get the same sort/filter bar: `{{< wintable filter="FV,Team" >}}` or `{{< board ... filter="POS,FV" >}}` adds a dropdown per named column, plus an "age ≤" box when the table has an Age column.

## College Basketball ARKBOARD

`static/data/cbb-all.json` is the 2026-27 preseason board: every player on an ESPN D-I roster, graded on the 20-80 FV scale and rendered by `{{< cbbboard >}}` (filters, sortable columns, a scouting report per player, and a season select that shows past preseason boards from `static/data/cbb-past.json` with each player's NBA outcome). `{{< cbbfv >}}` draws the measured FV scale and `{{< cbbfv view="model" >}}` the drivers kept, both from `data/cbb.json`.

```sh
node scripts/cbb_on3.mjs 2026 2027      # On3 industry rankings (playwright, as for fetch_cfb.mjs); history: 2009 on
node scripts/cbb_dpm.mjs 2010 2026      # databallr DPM by NBA season (public stats page, playwright)
python3 scripts/cbb_link.py             # Barttorvik careers 2008-26 -> NBA careers (Basketball-Reference) + On3/247 grades
python3 scripts/cbb_fit.py              # driver test, ladder fit, FV scale, backtests -> data/cbb_fit.json, static/data/cbb-past.json
python3 scripts/fetch_cbb.py            # ESPN rosters + Barttorvik 2026-27 rosters -> the board (--offline: from the cache)
python3 scripts/cbb_live_backtest.py    # in-season blend weights from Barttorvik game logs 2009-10 on -> data/cbb_live_fit.json
python3 scripts/cbb_live.py             # in season: this season to date blended into the board (daily, cbb-board.yml)
python3 scripts/nba_xpm.py 2010 2026   # NBA XPM leaderboard by season; --current for the season in progress (daily, nba-xpm.yml)
```

In season (from Nov 1) the `cbb board` workflow runs `scripts/cbb_live.py` every morning: each player's preseason grade and the grade his career gets with this season's Barttorvik line to date are blended by minutes played, w = minutes / (minutes + k), k backtested per newcomers and returners; the drivers, P(NBA) and xBPM move the same way, and the board is regraded. Its prior is `scripts/cbb_prior.json.gz`, the preseason board as `fetch_cbb.py` graded it (written by every `fetch_cbb.py` run, so commit it with a rebuild). `fetch_cbb.py` refuses a fresh pull once the season starts, because Barttorvik's 2026-27 file then holds this season's lines. `{{< cbblive >}}` shows the blend's backtest; `data/cbb_live.json` holds the in-season summary.

Raw pulls are cached in `scripts/.cache/cbb/` (Barttorvik `getadvstats.php` CSVs, Basketball-Reference draft and advanced pages, 247 composite pages, On3, ESPN, DPM). The model: realized FV = the NBA role a college career reached (`TIERS` in `cbb_link.py`: All-NBA, All-Star, seasons as a starter, rotation seasons, NBA games); E[FV] = 20 + 10 x P(FV 30+) + ... + 10 x P(70+), one ridge logistic per rung, fit on the 2009-16 preseason boards; newcomers and returners fit apart; a driver stays only if it lowers out-of-sample deviance in 4 of 5 folds. FV assigns the scarcity counts (players per board who reached each role) in E[FV] order. 2017-26 boards are the forward test, scored on draft slot.

## 1NT/LAURSEN_111: ESEA and FACEIT

`content/laursen_111.md` has two tabs, **1NT** (first) and **LAURSEN_111**. Any tab on the site opens from the URL hash (`/laursen_111/#laursen-111`, `/arkboard/college-football/#draft-board`); old `/arkboard/#…` links forward to the right sport page, and `static/_redirects` adds short links like `/1nt`.

`data/esea.json` is written by `scripts/fetch_esea.mjs` from the FACEIT Data API and rendered by `{{< esea part="..." >}}`:

- `season`, `matches`, `cards`, `players`: the S59 record, standings rank, every match with both teams' box scores (country flags, ARK Rating, swing, map MVP), player cards and season stats. GFX buttons on the season window and each box score make 16:9 graphics.
- `preview`: the next opponent (roster, elo, S59 results and ARK Ratings), map edges (team map win rates shrunk to 50% by 8 maps, log5) a Bo1 veto by minimax with 1NT always banning Inferno then Mirage (`FIXED_BANS`), and a round spread: elo, the expected map and team ARK Rating blended in log-odds into a map win chance, then turned into a per-round win chance and expected round differential with an exact race-to-13 model (MR3 overtime).
- `stakes`: the playoff line (top 48 per Main group), move-ups (top 4 to Advanced, top 2 to the Season Finals), move-downs (under 4 wins) and the Finals prize table, with binomial odds.
- `alltime`: every player's maps for 1NT (from each player's FACEIT match stats, cached and fetched incrementally), with tenure and the seasons/events played.
- `split` (LAURSEN_111 tab): his FACEIT maps split into PUGs, scrims (queue/hub matches with five 1NT members on his side) and 1NT matches (ESEA plus cups), with per-map and per-season tables.

- `form`: every player's last 100 FACEIT maps in 90 days, with All Games / Official (league, cups, qualifiers) / PUGs / Scrims tabs. Talent is 1-5 stars in half steps on the same rolling window (form ARK .70, elo .30; 3 stars = an average player in our league's matches, one star = one SD). The preview's team talent, role matchups, game plan, fanGFX and teamGFX use it.

- Every player gets Entry+, Duel+, Multi+, Clutch+, Util+, AWP+ (AWP impact vs everyone) and Surv+ on the rolling table and player cards, plus a hot/cold flag (ARK over the last 20 maps vs 100). Team talent is the projected five's mean stars, shown next to each team.
- `stakes` adds a 10,000-run season simulation (seed odds, top 4/16/48, the cut line); `model` (`model_log.exe`) freezes each preview's projection at kickoff and scores it (calls, covers, Brier); `lineups` shows map W-L by five-man group and pair (official and scrims, 90 days); the preview's veto dropdown shows the opponent's official maps (90 days). One-match lineups go in `data/cs.json` `matchLineups` (by match id).

Round-level data (pistols, eco/anti-eco, setups, positions) needs match demos, which this API key can't download (FACEIT's Downloads API needs separate approval). `ESEA_DEMO_PROBE` (the workflow's `probe` input) checks access.

ARK Rating 2 fits its weights on round outcomes instead of hand-setting them: over every cached map (both teams' box scores, 90 days), each team's round share is regressed (ridge toward prior weights, sign-constrained, clutches capped) on its players' per-round kills, deaths, damage, multi-kill rounds, entry ±, clutches, assists, enemies flashed, utility damage and flash/utility successes minus the other team's. A player's value is the rounds he adds per round; a KAST-style outcome credit shares half the team's round share over .500 among its five (half equally, half by round MVPs). 1.00 = the average line in our matches, spread matched to HLTV Rating 1.0. `data/esea.json` arkModel has the weights, fit size and R². `rankings` (S59 rankings) lists every team in the division with its standing, round differential, team ARK, talent stars, elo and a power rank.

It needs a FACEIT server API key (developers.faceit.com) in `FACEIT_API_KEY`, a repository secret for `.github/workflows/esea.yml`, which runs every 20 minutes and commits only when something changed. `ESEA_SEASON` moves it to the next season (it otherwise follows `season` in the data file, starting at 59).
