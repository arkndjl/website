NFL WAR is wins above replacement: what a player has added to his team this season over a freely available one (a late-round or undrafted player, 0.4 AV a season). it's (AV - replacement) x .108, about 3.8 points of margin an AV and 35.5 points a win. AV is Approximate Value, rebuilt each week from play stats, snaps and scoring the way Pro Football Reference builds it (team offense and defense pools split by role and production) and fit to PFR's own AV: it tracks PFR at r .91 on a single season of rookies it never saw. pick a position for its leaderboard, or per 17 games for the rate view.
