{{< winpane title="eephus.io.exe" icon="eephus" status="spring 2026 - present" class="desk__wide" >}}
**[eephus.io](https://eephus.io)** :: founder and lead developer. a sabermetrics site built by six college students, beta v1.0. [full write-up](/eephus/).

- i built the site architecture, the static build, the data model and the retro card and newspaper design.
- [PROSPX](https://eephus.io/prospx/): consensus 20-80 FV and tool grades for every prospect in affiliated baseball, charted over time like a stock price, with farm system grades, transactions, the injured list, draft, international and overseas boards, a time machine and scatterplots.
- eFV: my Future Value model grounded in what prospects went on to do. a ridge WAR projection on a leakage-clean feature matrix, put on the 20-80 scale, with surplus value. backtested out of sample against consensus, FanGraphs and MLB Pipeline.
- MLB modules: [projections](https://eephus.io/projections/) (five-year bWAR paths with floor, mean and ceiling), [free agency](https://eephus.io/fa/) (market value, rational value and team fits, 12 past winters), [MLB stats](https://eephus.io/mlbstats/) with my REM+, RIV+, SEM+ and HEM+ metrics, [Pitcher Alchemy Lab](https://eephus.io/pal/) (starter and reliever role conversions), [trade machine](https://eephus.io/trademachine/), [fantasy](https://eephus.io/fantasy/) and [CollegeSavant](https://eephus.io/collegesavant/) (college to pro translation, CS+).
- a glossary and model registry every number on the site resolves to, misses included.
- timeline: v0.1 closed alpha May 28, 2026, tested by prospect writers and people who work for MLB teams; open beta summer 2026; beta v1.0 now.
- data: MLB Stats API, Statcast, Baseball-Reference.

{{< winpane title="arkndjl.net.exe" icon="arkboard" status="2025 - present" class="desk__wide" >}}
**arkndjl.net** :: my site. i build and run all of it: Hugo, Python and Node data pipelines, and GitHub workflows that refresh the boards, odds and news on a schedule.

- [ARKBOARD](/arkboard/): player boards for college football, college basketball, the NFL, NBA, NHL, hockey prospects, soccer, soccer prospects, tennis, Counter-Strike 2 and baseball. Future Value on the 20-80 scale is the mean outcome of past players with the same profile, with outcome odds in each player's card.
- pro models: NFL WAR, NBA and college XPM, NHL WAR and goalie GSAx, soccer GAR, ARK Ranking power ratings, game models and season odds from simulated seasons in every sport.
- college market: xNIL (what a player is worth), yNIL (what he is paid, from reported school budgets), a ledger of confirmed NIL deals, an agencies board ranking NIL agencies on their clients, the transfer portal with Transfer%, Ret%, and DECT, which puts declare, college and transfer in dollars for every draft prospect.
- recruits: ARK stars and FV for every recruit, class rankings with their own GFX, and high school ARK stars graded back to the 2008 class for 27,937 college football and 5,561 college basketball players.
- drafts: NFL and NBA mock drafts (predictive, blended, conviction) with trades.
- [predictions](/arkboard-predictions/): every game model against the market, picks sized by Kelly on calibrated edge, each judged against the closing line.
- method: every model change is tested out of sample and ships only if it beats the current model held out. [ARKLOG](/arklog/) has every change since May 2026: 1,266 changes and 429 tests, 170 shipped, 173 part shipped, 86 failed. [Process](/arkboard/process/) holds the method, a glossary of every metric with its numbers read from the live fits, and the research.
- also on the site: [1NT](/1nt/) and [LAURSEN_111](/laursen_111/) (my Counter-Strike team and stream), [ST. CLAIRE](/stclaire/) (music, art, wave), [ARKLIBRARY](/arklibrary/), a news feed written from the site's data, and site search.
- [writing](/posts/): sabermetrics, prospects, the Mets and economics, including [introducing XPM](/posts/introducing-xpm/), [introducing college XPM](/posts/introducing-cbb-xpm/), [aWOBA](/posts/awoba-intro/) and [The State Of Analytics Across Team Sports](/posts/state-of-analytics-across-team-sports/).
