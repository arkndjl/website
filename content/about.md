{{< winpane title="eephus.io.exe" icon="eephus" status="spring 2026 - present" class="desk__wide" >}}
**[eephus.io](https://eephus.io) / MLB PROSPX** :: founder, builder. sabermetrics site and an all-in-one prospect / farm system evaluation tool. [full write-up](/eephus/).

- **consensus grades**: industry sources combined into consensus 20-80 FV and tool grades for every prospect in affiliated baseball (3,135 at the alpha launch), charted over time like a stock price, with promotions, trades and wide source disagreement marked.
- **metrics (Arkmetrics)**: WAG (WAR above grade, production against the expectation a prospect's grade set), RPS+ (performance against same-age peers), SC+ (Statcast composite, 100 = pool average), Tool+, DvM+ and 6YDvM+ development indices, surplus value, ETA projections from performance, level, age, injury history and each org's promotion speed.
- **farm systems**: every organization graded on acquisition, development, finances and production, with six-year trajectories, level depth, spend mix and ROI.
- **boards and tools**: MLB draft, international amateur and overseas boards; transactions, injured list, leaderboards; build-your-own scatterplots with least-squares trendline and R²; glossary for every metric.
- **data**: MLB Stats API, Statcast.
- **timeline**: v0.1 closed alpha May 28, 2026, tested by prospect writers and people who work for MLB teams; open beta summer 2026; live now with MLB projections.

{{< winpane title="arkndjl.net.exe" icon="arkboard" status="2025 - present" class="desk__wide" >}}
**arkndjl.net** :: portfolio, blog and the home of ARKBOARD. Hugo site, Python and Node data pipelines, scheduled refreshes in season.

- **[ARKBOARD](/arkboard/)**: prospect boards on the 20-80 Future Value scale, where FV is the expected peak surplus value over replacement of past prospects with the same profile, as a share of a team (one scale in every sport), with outcome odds and ceiling in each player's dropdown.
  - **[football](/arkboard-college-football/)**: every FBS + FCS player graded, 2027 NFL draft board and three mock drafts (predictive, blended, conviction), transfer portal and Transfer%, NIL market value (xNIL), recruits, team builder, coaching staffs, game projections and season odds.
  - **[basketball](/arkboard-college-basketball/)**: every D-I player and the international prospects graded against peak NBA impact, FV drivers backtested on NBA careers, 2027 NBA mocks, NCAA tournament odds from bracket and conference-tournament sims.
  - **[hockey](/arkboard-hockey-prospects/)** and **[soccer](/arkboard-soccer-prospects/)** prospects: the 2027 NHL draft class and drafted players (fitted NHLe, backtested on the 2008-19 classes); every under-22 in 33 soccer leagues (backtested on 2013-20).
  - **[baseball](/arkboard-baseball/)**: high school and college draft boards.
- **pro models**: [NFL](/arkboard-nfl/) WAR, [NBA](/arkboard-nba/) XPM and contract value, [NHL](/arkboard-nhl/) WAR and goalie GSAx, [soccer](/arkboard-soccer/) ratings across 18 leagues; game projections, power rankings (ARK Ranking) and season odds from simulated seasons in every sport.
- **[predictions](/arkboard-predictions/)**: every game model against the market, picks sized in Dengler Units (Kelly on calibrated edge), each judged against the closing line; futures, win totals, player props.
- **method**: every model change is backtested out of sample and ships only if it beats the current model held out; failed tests are logged, not shipped.
- **[writing](/posts/)**: sabermetrics, prospects, the Mets and economics, including [aWOBA](/posts/awoba-intro/), [an NBA Future Value grading system](/posts/nba-future-value-grading-system/), [The State Of Analytics Across Team Sports](/posts/state-of-analytics-across-team-sports/) and [Sabermetrics As A Microcosm](/posts/sabermetrics-microcosm/).
