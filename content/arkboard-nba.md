**XPM** is the ARKBOARD's NBA impact number: points per 100 possessions over an average player, the 50/50 blend of databallr's DPM and Basketball-Reference's BPM. O-XPM and D-XPM blend each side the same way, and VORX is VORP on XPM, (XPM + 2) x MP / 19,680. In a test on 480 team-seasons (2011-26, ratings from earlier seasons predicting each team's margin) the blend beat DPM and BPM alone, and nothing we built beat the blend. The College Basketball board grades prospects on peak XPM.
