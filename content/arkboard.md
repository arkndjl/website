ARKBOARD = draft prospect big boards, graded on the 20–80 Future Value (FV) scale. FV = expected peak surplus value over replacement, measured as a share of an average team's: the best three seasons from 22 to 28, averaged over what past prospects with the same profile became, busts and stars included, so it sits below the best case. One scale in every sport: 20 replacement, 40 = 2% of a team, 50 = 5% (an average regular), 60 = 13%, 70 = 32%, 80 = 80% (an MVP peak); a 55 is worth the same in football, basketball, hockey and soccer. Tiers every 5 points, grades in steps of 1. each player's dropdown shows the range: the odds of each outcome and the ceiling. one page per sport; each with its own boards, models and FV conversions. scale and baseball -> basketball conversion: [Creating An NBA Future Value Grading System](/posts/nba-future-value-grading-system/). MiLB prospects (post-draft): MLB PROSPX on [eephus.io](https://eephus.io).
